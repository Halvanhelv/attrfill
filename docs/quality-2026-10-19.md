## Speed

Without deduplication, the warm pipeline processed identical cards at roughly **250 cards/s**.
After deduplication within a work batch, `Extraction::Runner` handled the same scenario at
**3130 cards/s** (`1000` cards in `0.32` s, after warm-up), i.e. roughly **12.5 times faster**.

The optimisation is especially useful for products repeated across different files. Row order and the original `id`s
are preserved; unique cards are still processed independently.
